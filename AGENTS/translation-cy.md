# Welsh (cy-gb, cy-001) terminology

Welsh text follows the Welsh Government's **BydTermCymru** resources (<https://www.gov.wales/bydtermcymru>):

- **TermCymru**, the termbase (about 78,000 entries, Open Government Licence). The full CSV is on <https://gov.wales/download-termcymru>; columns are `Saesneg, Cymraeg, ..., Statws (A/B/C), Pwnc`. Prefer a term with status A, then B, then C, and prefer a Health, Finance and statistics, or General entry over one from another subject.
- **Yr Arddulliadur**, the style guide: open punctuation, figures for numbers 11 and above (and always for money, percentages and measurements), decimal point not vigesimal for decimals, abbreviations written in full on first use, `etc` without a full stop.
- **Scientific loanword spelling guidelines** for new technical terms (adapt spelling to Welsh: `c`→`s` before `e`/`i`, `ph`→`ff`, `-ic`→`-ig`, `-ium`→`-iwm`, `-ide`→`-id`).
